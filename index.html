Asheville & Western North Carolina

A good night starts with live music.

Find a show, discover a room, and make a plan. AVL.LIVE brings local live music listings together so you can explore what is happening around Asheville.

Explore upcoming shows ↗
01 / FIND A SHOW

Start with your date.

Open the show listings and look for a night that works for you. Explore artists you know, then leave room for something unfamiliar.

02 / PICK A ROOM

Discover the venue.

Browse the venue directory to discover places to hear music. Compare the location with your plans for dinner, transport, and the rest of your evening.

03 / CHECK DETAILS

Confirm before you go.

Use the venue or ticket seller’s official listing to confirm doors, set times, age restrictions, accessibility, ticket availability, and any last-minute changes.

Build a little flexibility into your night.

Doors and performance times can differ. Allow time for parking or a ride, keep your ticket handy, and check the organizer’s latest update before leaving. If a show is sold out, browse other nearby listings and discover a new favorite.
